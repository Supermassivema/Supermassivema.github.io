Sid Motion has announced she will wind up the London gallery she founded in 2016, which moved from King's Cross to the Penarth Centre in South Bermondsey in 2019. The final exhibition, *That Which Appears*, runs from 18 September to 31 October 2026 and will be the gallery's 85th; it then travels to Sheffield's Millennium Gallery in April 2028. Motion described the decision as a deliberate one made from a position of strength, while also acknowledging the gallery could not financially do justice to what its artists were capable of. The closure follows Stephen Friedman Gallery's collapse in February 2026 and Pace's decision in June to cut its roster by around 50 artists.

A gallery closing while still solvent is a different signal from one closing in debt. It suggests the pressure on mid-sized galleries is now built into how the market works rather than a passing cash-flow problem.
