Event Saturday, March 28, 2026

Opening Reception: ART SPACE BYRON Inaugural Exhibition

Join us on Saturday, March 28 for the opening reception of ART SPACE BYRON's inaugural exhibition, featuring works by Geum Sahong, Debbie Doye, and Stephen Jones. The exhibition brings together East Asian and local artistic perspectives in Inverell's newest contemporary art space. Light refreshments will be served. All are welcome.

Exhibition: 2 March – 5 June 2026
Opening Reception: Saturday, 28 March 2026
Venue: ART SPACE BYRON, 44 Byron St, Inverell NSW 2360
