You're invited. On **Saturday, 28 March 2026**, ART SPACE BYRON hosts the opening reception for our inaugural exhibition — an evening to meet the artists and see the works together for the first time.

It's an open, informal gathering, and all are welcome. Throughout the year we'll also host artist talks, workshops, and community events — follow us on Instagram to hear about them first.
