ART SPACE BYRON is growing. We're looking for two people to join our team in our heritage building at 44 Byron Street, Inverell — helping regional audiences connect with Australian and Korean contemporary art.

## Gallery Assistant

A welcoming and organised person to support the daily running of the gallery and our exhibitions, events and workshops.

**Your duties will include**

- Welcoming visitors and providing information about artists and exhibitions
- Assisting with artwork enquiries, sales and record keeping
- Supporting exhibition preparation, artwork handling and packing under supervision
- Assisting with artist talks, workshops and opening events
- Updating exhibition information, mailing lists and social media
- Maintaining a tidy, accessible and welcoming gallery
- General administrative support to the gallery team

**About you:** friendly communication and customer service skills, attention to detail and basic computer skills, an interest in art and community engagement, and a reliable, collaborative approach. Gallery, retail, hospitality or events experience is helpful, and transferable skills are welcome. Availability for occasional weekend or evening events would be valued.

**To apply:** your CV and a short introduction covering your interest in the role, relevant experience and availability.

[Download the full position description (Word)](/assets/files/jobs/gallery-assistant.docx)

## Gallery Curator

Working with the Gallery Director to develop exhibitions and public programs — including temporary, site-responsive installations that respond to the building's history, architecture and local connections.

**You will**

- Research and develop exhibition concepts and interpretive content
- Identify and liaise with artists regarding proposed exhibitions
- Coordinate artwork selection, loans, documentation and display requirements
- Work with artists and installation specialists to prepare exhibitions
- Develop artist talks, workshops and educational activities
- Contribute to exhibition publications, funding proposals and project reports

**About you:** qualifications or experience in curating, art history, visual arts or a related field; strong research, writing and organisational skills; an interest in contemporary art, installation practice and regional audiences; the ability to work respectfully with artists from diverse cultural backgrounds; and an understanding of artwork handling and presentation. Experience with international collaborations or heritage spaces is welcome.

**To apply:** your CV, a brief cover letter and examples of relevant exhibitions, projects or writing, with your availability and preferred working arrangements.
