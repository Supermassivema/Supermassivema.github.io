Shinsegae Group has signed on as headline partner of Frieze Seoul for the five years from 2026 to 2030, becoming the first Korean retail company to hold the fair's top sponsorship position. The fifth edition runs from 2 to 5 September 2026 at COEX in Gangnam, with more than 125 galleries from around 30 countries taking part. The group plans to build a dedicated lounge for visitors at the fair, and says it will help Korean artists and galleries reach overseas markets. Financial terms of the agreement were not made public.

The shift is worth noting because the slot has previously gone to international sponsors. Domestic capital committing for five years suggests the fair's place in Seoul is now settled rather than provisional.
