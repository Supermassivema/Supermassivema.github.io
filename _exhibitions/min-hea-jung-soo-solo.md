A solo exhibition by Min Hea Jung Soo. Her work transforms hazy memories into vivid imagery through details of birds, flowers, bees, and leaves — abstract from afar, yet strikingly clear up close. This exhibition brings her distinctive blend of Western painting technique and Eastern sensibility to Inverell.
