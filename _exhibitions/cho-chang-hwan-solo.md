A solo exhibition by Cho Chang-hwan. He visually manifests "breath" — the essential element of life — through the "spatialization of time." Using a single Lespedeza stalk, he layers strokes one by one onto the canvas, mirroring the ceaseless rhythm of respiration. This exhibition presents his SOOM (Breath) series in Inverell.
