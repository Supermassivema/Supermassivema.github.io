A solo exhibition by Lee Sang-kwon. His work examines the weight and depth of time through nature, reconstructing familiar landscapes with complex colour layers and brushstrokes. Through vivid and varied colours, he evokes sensations beyond reality, inviting viewers to project their own memories and emotions.
